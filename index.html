Meet the four desserts people hunt for.

01/ 04

Trileçe

Soaked sponge under a mirror of caramel.

A cloud of sponge drenched in three milks, layered with sweet cream and vanilla, then sealed under glossy caramel piped with cream. Every forkful is soft, cold and dangerously silky.

    02/ 04

    Chocolate Balls

    Rolled by hand. Gone by the handful.

    Rich cocoa and crushed biscuit, bound with chocolate and milk, rolled one by one in snowy coconut or a burst of rainbow sprinkles. Nobody stops at one.

      03/ 04

      Layali Beirut

      A Beirut night, set in cream.

      Silky semolina milk pudding under clouds of sweet cream, crushed pistachios and dried rose petals: the dessert of Beirut evenings, made the way it should be.

        04/ 04

        Cheesecake

        One silky base. Five moods.

        A buttery biscuit base, a cool, velvety cream-cheese middle, and the topping you choose: wild blueberry, sunny mango, ruby strawberry, Lotus crumble or deep chocolate.
