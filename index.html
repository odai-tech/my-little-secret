Trileçe

A soft cloud under a mirror of golden caramel.

Feather-light sponge soaked in three kinds of milk, layered with sweet vanilla cream and crowned with glossy caramel. Every forkful is cool, tender and melts the moment it touches your tongue.

    Chocolate Balls

    Rolled by hand, made for sharing (or not).

    Rich cocoa and crunchy biscuit, blended with chocolate and milk, then rolled one by one in snowy coconut or a happy burst of rainbow sprinkles. One is never enough.

      Layali Beirut

      A Beirut evening, set in cream.

      Silky semolina milk pudding under clouds of sweet cream, finished with crushed pistachios and delicate rose petals: the beloved dessert of Beirut nights, made with all the love it deserves.
